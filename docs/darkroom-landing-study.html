The workflow

Less time finding.
More time editing.

Your folders are the starting point. Darkroom reads the photos on your computer and keeps your edits separate from the original pixels.

01

Choose from the whole shoot.

Browse a photo grid, add ratings and color labels, then filter the library to the frames you want to edit.

02

Work on the final image.

Adjust light, color, and crop. Use masks for local adjustments and check the original as you work.

03

Export to your own folders.

Choose JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, or TIFF. Set the size and save the finished image to disk.

A few useful answers.

Which files can I open?

Darkroom opens Nikon NEF, JPEG, PNG, and WebP. Nikon NEF is the supported RAW format today.

Does it upload my photos?

No. Darkroom reads photos from local storage. The optional subject and sky masks need a model download, then run on your computer.

What happens to the original?

Develop adjustments do not replace the original pixels. Darkroom stores the edits separately and writes a finished image when you export.

Where do the exported images go?

To the folder you choose on your computer. Export JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, or TIFF at the size you need.
