Reasonable Questions

Darkroom is early software. These 4 answers mark what it does today.

Does Darkroom Upload Photos?

No. Darkroom reads photos from local storage. The optional subject and sky tools download their model files once, then run on your machine.

Which Camera Files Work Today?

Nikon NEF is the supported RAW format. Darkroom also opens JPEG, PNG, and WebP. Other RAW formats are not supported yet.

What Happens to the Original?

Develop edits do not replace the source pixels. Darkroom keeps its edit history separately and writes the finished image only when you export.

Can a Batch Survive a Restart?

Yes. Darkroom persists batch jobs in the local catalog and can resume work that did not finish before the app closed.
